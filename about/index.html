About Decision Arcade

Welcome to Decision Arcade. At its core, Decision Arcade is a free, browser-based decision-making tool featuring three core modes designed to help you break through analysis paralysis and get on with your life.

Why It Exists

We believe that decision fatigue is real. Modern life bombards us with a seemingly endless and exhausting sequence of micro-choices—from deciding what to watch on a streaming platform, to endlessly debating where to order takeout. Sometimes, you simply need a push (or a mirror to reflect what you subconsciously wanted all along). We built this application because every single 'should I...' text sent to a friend deserves a faster, less stressful answer.

How It Works

Decision Arcade helps you choose by offering three distinct methods to tackle your dilemma:

  • Fate: Pure, deterministic randomness. The system flips the coin for you when you just need an objective answer.
  • Instinct: A rapid-fire, timed gut-check quiz designed to trick your brain into revealing what you actually want before overthinking sets in.
  • Duel: A head-to-head bracket elimination system that pits your options against each other until one ultimate winner remains.

Our Philosophy

We fundamentally believe that everyday decisions don't have to be stressful. In our experience, the best choices are often made quickly and decisively. Decision Arcade is intentionally designed to make deciding fun — or at the very least, less agonizing. Why should a helpful utility feel sterile? We wanted this to feel engaging, dynamic, and genuinely helpful.

What We Don't Do

Your privacy matters immensely. Because of this, Decision Arcade uses no accounts, no forced sign-ups, no data collection, no AI generation, and absolutely no behavioral tracking. Your decisions and inputs stay firmly on your own device.

Who It's For

This tool was built for anyone who has ever stared at a restaurant menu for 20 minutes, texted three separate friends 'should I buy this...', or flipped a coin and then immediately ignored it to go with the other option anyway. It's the ultimate tool for chronic hesitators.

Built With Care

Decision Arcade is proudly hand-coded. We use no heavy JavaScript frameworks or bloated libraries. We focus on fast-loading, semantic HTML, and lean vanilla Javascript to ensure it works instantly on any device.

Ready to Choose?

Try the decision maker for a customized experience, use the yes or no generator for a quick verdict, figure out what to eat tonight, or start a duel between two options.
