How the Yes or No Generator Works

Sometimes you don't need a nuanced explanation or a four-part quiz — you just need a straightforward binary answer. That's exactly what the Yes or No Generator provides. When your brain refuses to cooperate and you're stuck in an endless loop of "what if," forcing a simple YES or NO can instantly break the deadlock.

Behind the scenes, we use a deterministic hash algorithm. This means we take the exact letters of your question and run them through a math formula to generate a number. If it's even, it's YES. If it's odd, it's NO. Because it's deterministic, the same question will always get the same answer. It's designed purely for fun and to provide a quick spark of decision-making momentum, not for divination or serious life choices!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the answer really random?

It's deterministic — the same question always gets the same answer via a hash algorithm. Different wording = different answer.

Can I get a different answer?

Rephrase your question slightly. Since the answer is based on the exact text, even a small change (like adding punctuation) will give a different result.

Should I actually follow this advice?

This is for entertainment only. For important decisions, talk to a real person you trust. Do not make major life choices based on a web app.

Why does my friend get a different answer?

They might be wording the question differently. Share your exact link or type the exact same string of letters to show them your result.
