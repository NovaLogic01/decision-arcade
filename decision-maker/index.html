How Decision Arcade Works

Decision-making fatigue is real — psychologists call it 'decision fatigue' and studies show adults make over 35,000 decisions daily. Sometimes the hardest ones aren't the big life-changers but the everyday choices that pile up. Decision Arcade gives you three different ways to cut through the noise.

Fate Mode — Leave It to the Universe

Fate uses a deterministic random algorithm seeded by your question text. That means the same question always gets the same answer — which makes results shareable and reproducible. It's not truly 'random' in the way a coin flip is; it's more like the universe assigning each question its own predetermined answer. The verdicts are category-aware: ask about food and you'll get food-themed responses.

Instinct Mode — Trust Your Gut

Instinct is a four-question quiz designed to surface what you're already leaning toward. It's NOT a personality test or psychological assessment — it's a mirror. Each question is designed to trigger your gut reaction, and by the end, the pattern usually reveals a clear lean. Research on 'thin-slicing' (rapid cognition) suggests your first instinct is often more reliable than extended deliberation for certain types of decisions.

Duel Mode — Head-to-Head Showdown

Duel is for when you've narrowed it down to two options but can't pick. Enter both, and Duel picks a winner using the same deterministic algorithm as Fate. The real magic is sharing — send the Duel link to a friend and they'll see 'Your friend has a decision' before the reveal. It's decisions as entertainment.

No sign-up, no data collection, no AI. Your decisions stay on your device in your browser's local storage. Use it for fun, use it to break a deadlock, or use it to prove to your friend that pizza was always the right call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Decision Arcade really free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no premium tier, no hidden costs. We're supported by ads.

How does the random decision maker work?

Fate mode uses a deterministic hash algorithm seeded by your question text. The same question always produces the same verdict, which means your results are reproducible and shareable. It's not truly random — think of it as the universe having a predetermined answer for every question.

Is the Instinct quiz a real psychological test?

No, and we don't claim it is. Instinct is a playful four-question exercise designed to surface your existing gut feeling. It's based on the idea that you usually already know what you want — you just need help hearing it. It's not clinically validated or based on any specific psychological framework.

Does Decision Arcade store my data?

Your last 20 decisions are saved in your browser's local storage — that means they stay on your device and are never sent to any server. You can clear your history at any time. We don't have accounts, databases, or any way to see what you've asked.

Can I share my results?

Yes! Every result has a Share button that uses your device's native sharing (on mobile) or copies a link to your clipboard (on desktop). Duel mode generates a unique shareable URL. You can also download a result card image to post on social media.

What should I use Decision Arcade for?

Anything from 'what should I eat for dinner' to 'should I text my ex' (please don't) to 'which movie should I watch tonight.' It's designed for everyday decisions, not life-or-death choices. Please don't use it to make medical, legal, or financial decisions.
