Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Welcome to Decision Arcade. We value your privacy and transparency above all else. This Privacy Policy is designed to clearly outline exactly what information we handle, what stays entirely on your own device, and how third-party services may interact with you while you use our tool. Please read this thoroughly.

What Data We Collect

Decision Arcade is built on a privacy-first philosophy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to any external server. We do not require or offer user accounts, which means there are absolutely no emails, no usernames, and no passwords required to use the service. What you type into the decision maker stays with you. Furthermore, no internal analytics tracking (such as Google Analytics or similar user behavior tracking software) is currently active on our servers for monitoring your usage of the site.

Local Storage

To improve your user experience and save you time, we use your browser's native `localStorage` mechanism to save your last 20 decisions for your convenience. This ensures you don't lose your place or your recent choices.

Critically, this data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server, nor can we access it. The stored data strictly includes: the text of your decision options, the specific mode used (such as Fate, Instinct, or Duel), the final result of the decision, and a timestamp for when the decision was generated.

You have full control over this data. You can clear it at any time using the dedicated 'Clear History' button within the decision maker tool itself, or by manually clearing your browser's site data in your browser settings. We also use `sessionStorage` to temporarily hold your current decision state while you navigate between screens. This temporary session data is automatically and permanently cleared when you close the tab or window.

Cookies

Decision Arcade itself, as a core application, does not set any first-party cookies on your device. We do not use cookies to track your behavior across the internet or manage accounts, as we do not have accounts. However, third-party advertising partners (detailed below) may set cookies for ad personalization, frequency capping, and ad performance measurement.

Third-Party Advertising

Decision Arcade is completely free for all users, and to keep it running, it is supported by advertising. We plan to use Google AdSense to display relevant ads. When this advertising system is active, Google and its partners may collect data as described in Google's privacy policy.

Google may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website or other websites across the internet. If you prefer not to see personalized ads, you have the right and ability to opt out of personalized advertising entirely by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

Third-Party Links

Our site may occasionally contain links to external sites that are not operated by us. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Children's Privacy

Decision Arcade is directed at a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information or data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data (which our system is not built to accept), please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted immediately on this page with an updated effective date at the top. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out. You can contact us at the email provided on our contact page.
