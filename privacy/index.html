Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy...oice paralysis is exhausting. Whether you're trying to figure out what to eat for dinner, if you should take that job offer, or who gets to pick the movie tonight, the mental energy spent deciding often outweighs the decision itself.

Most "random decision" apps are cluttered with pop-ups, require you to create an account, or feel like they were built in 1999. We wanted a clean, fast, reliable tool that you can just bookmark and use.

Our Philosophy

  • Zero Friction: No sign-ups, no logins, no paywalls. Ever.
  • Privacy First: Your decisions stay on your device. We use local storage, meaning your dilemmas are never sent to a database.
  • Deterministic Magic: When you use Fate mode, the universe (via our hashing algorithm) gives you an answer based on your exact wording. It's reproducible and meant to be shared.
