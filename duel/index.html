Duel — Two Options, One Winner

How Duel Works

When you've successfully narrowed down your choices to exactly two options, but still can't make the final call, it's time for a Duel. Simply enter both options, and our deterministic algorithm will pick the ultimate winner.

The real fun of Duel mode is sharing it. After you generate a result, you can copy the unique link and send it to a friend. When they open it, they won't see the winner immediately — they'll see the two options facing off, and they have to click to reveal what the universe decided. It turns everyday decisions into interactive entertainment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the winner random?

Yes and no. It uses a deterministic hash based on the exact text of both options. This means if you and your friend duel the exact same two words, you'll always get the same winner.

Does the order of options matter?

No, we combine the text in a way that generates a consistent hash, but it's best to use the exact share link if you want someone to see your specific outcome.

Can I save the result?

Yes! Every duel result is saved automatically to your local browser history (if you're on the main Decision Maker tool page) and you can always bookmark or share the URL.
